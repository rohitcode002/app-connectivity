It was informed that the transmission system for connectivity at Bikaner-V is identified and approved in 40th CMETS NR meeting held
on 12.09.2025. However, the scheme is still under approval by competent authority. Further, transmission system for connectivity at
Bhadla-V PS, Barmer-III PS & Sanchore PS is yet to be identified. Upon finalization, the transmission scheme associated with above
RE pooling stations shall be taken up for approval by competent authority. Due to the above reasons, intimation of final grant of
connectivity is not yet issued to connectivity applications associated with above applications under 5.2. Final grant of connectivity
shall be issued only upon approval of transmission scheme by competent authority. In view of the above, above applications under
5.2 shall be taken up for discussion & grant after issuance of final grant intimations and subsequent signing of connectivity agreement
by above applicants. Applicants noted the same.

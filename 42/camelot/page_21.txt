Even if it is considered that intimation shall be issued by the end of this month (Nov’25),  the SCoD cannot be beyond Nov’27 as per Regulations. Further, the above grant shall be subject to availability of Transmission system for connectivity granted to App. No. 2200000210 (300 MW).

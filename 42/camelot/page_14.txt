| I   I   M   I   U It  was  informed  that  M/s AM  Green  Energy  Private  Limited  (AMGEPL)  has  applied  for  addition  of  generation  capacity  (100MW  Solar  &  125  MW  BESS)  under  regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations, 2022 within the same connectivity quantum earlier granted at Bikaner-IV PS under App. No. 2200000319 (300 MW). Accordingly,  it was proposed to grant addition of generation capacity (125 MW BESS & 100 MW Solar) under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations.  t was also informed that, the net injection at any point of time shall not exceed the quantum of total Connectivity granted to the existing Connectivity grantee i.e.,  M/s AMGEPL (Appl. No. 2200000319 for 300 MW). Further, M/s AMGEPL (Appl. No. 2200000319) shall be responsible for compliance with the Grid Code and other  regulations of the Central  Commission for the above  additional generation capacity as ’Lead generator’  in  terms of clause (y)(ii) of Regulation 2.1. Further, M/s  AMGEPL shall submit the technical connection data i.r.o. above additional capacity for checking necessary compliances in timely manner so as to provide sufficient  time for ensuring necessary compliances.   t was also informed that M/s AMGEPL has submitted the SCoD of additional generation capacity from 31.12.2027. However, as per the regulations 5.2 (g), SCoD  of additional capacity shall not be greater than 24 months from the date of issuance of in-principle grant. Even if it is considered that intimation shall be issued by  the end of this month (Nov’25), the SCoD cannot be beyond 24 months from the date of issuance of in-principle intimation i.e. Nov’27 as per Regulations. Accordingly,  M/s AMGEPL was asked to prepone the SCoD date of additional capacity and inform a suitable date within the timeline as per regulations.   /s AMGEPL  in  the  meeting  requested  to  align  the  SCoD  date  with  24  months  from  the  date  of  issuance  of  in-principle  intimation  in  line  with  the  regulations.  Considering the same, it was agreed to grant the above capacity addition under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations with SCoD of 24 months from date of in-principle  intimation. Further, the above grant shall be subject to availability of Transmission system for connectivity granted to App. No. 2200000319 (300 MW). M/s AMGEPL  noted and agreed for the same.  t was also informed that M/s AMGEPL need to comply with the requirements mentioned in Reg. 37.10(d) (i to iv) as below within the timelines as per regulations:  (i)  SCoD  (ii)  Conn BG1 Rs. 50 lakh & Conn BG3 of Rs. 2 lakh/MW as per 5.2(e)  (iii)  Documents as per 5.2(h)  (iv)  Maximum drawl from the grid required for BESS  pon compliance as  per (i), (iii)  & (iv)  above, in-principle  intimation  for grant of  connectivity to additional  capacity (100 MW  Solar  &  125 MW  BESS) within the  connectivity quantum shall be issued. Further, as informed earlier, the maximum drawl permitted for BESS shall be intimated at the later stage (with requirement of  additional Conn BGs (if applicable)). M/s AMGEPL noted the same. |

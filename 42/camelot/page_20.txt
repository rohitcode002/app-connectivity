| I   M   M M/s Solarcraft Power India 4 Private Limited (SPI4PL) has applied for addition of generation capacity (150 MW BESS) under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations,  2022 within the same connectivity quantum earlier granted at 220 kV Bhadla-II PS under App. No. 2200000152(150 MW)). Accordingly, it was proposed to grant  addition of generation capacity (50 MW BESS) under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations.   t was also informed that, the net injection at any point of time shall not exceed the quantum of total Connectivity granted to the existing Connectivity grantee i.e.,  M/s SPI4PL (Appl. No. 2200000152 for 150 MW). Further, M/s SPI4PL (Appl. No. 2200000152) shall be responsible for compliance with the Grid Code and other  regulations of the Central Commission for the above additional generation capacity as ’Lead generator’ in terms of clause (y)(ii) of Regulation 2.1. Further, M/s  SPI4PL shall submit the technical connection data i.r.o. above additional capacity for checking necessary compliances in timely manner so as to provide sufficient  time for ensuring necessary compliances.   /s SPI4PL has requested SCoD of additional generation capacity from 31.03.2028. However, as per the regulations 5.2 (g), SCoD of additional capacity shall not  be greater than 24 months from the date of issuance of in-principle grant. Even if it is considered that intimation shall be issued by the end of this month (Nov’25),  the SCoD cannot be beyond Nov’27 as per Regulations. Accordingly, M/s SPI4PL was asked to prepone the SCoD date of additional capacity and inform a suitable  date within the timeline as per regulations.   /s  SPI4PL  in  the  meeting  requested  to  align  the  SCoD  date  with  24  months  from  the  date  of  issuance  of  in-principle  intimation  in  line  with  the  regulations.  Considering the same, it was agreed to grant the above capacity addition under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations with SCoD of 24 months from date of in-principle  intimation. Further, the above grant shall be subject to availability of Transmission system for connectivity granted to App. No. 2200000152 (150 MW).

Accordingly, it was proposed to grant addition of generation capacity (240 MW Solar & 300 MW BESS) under regulation 5.2 of GNA Regulations. It may be noted  that M/s AHPPL has provided different start dates for solar & BESS capacity. However, for a single application there cannot be multiple dates. Therefore, M/s AHPPL  was asked to clarify regarding the same & inform the single date for the project. It was also suggested that in case AHPPL wants separate start dates for Solar &  BESS capacity, M/s AHPPL is required to file separate applications under 5.2.  /s AHPPL informed that they have submitted SCoD of 31.03.2027 as per 3rd amendment & the same may be considered as SCoD of both Solar and BESS capacity.  The same was noted.  t was also informed that the net injection at any point of time shall not exceed the quantum of total Connectivity granted to the existing Connectivity grantee i.e., M/s  AHPPL  (Appl.  No.  1200002471  for  300  MW).  Further,  M/s AHPPL  (Appl.  No.  1200002471)  shall  be  responsible  for  compliance  with  the  Grid  Code  and  other  regulations of the Central  Commission for the above  additional generation capacity as ’Lead generator’  in  terms of clause (y)(ii) of Regulation 2.1.

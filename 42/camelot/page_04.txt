| Minutes of 42nd Consultation Meeting for Evolving Transmission Schemes in Northern Region held on 11.11.2025                                                             |
| It was informed that, the minutes of 41st CMETS NR meeting held on 10.10.2025 were circulated vide CTU letter dated 06.11.2025. No comments were                         |
| received by CTUIL regarding the same. Accordingly, minutes of 41st CMETS NR meeting is confirmed as circulated. The 42nd CMETS NR meeting was                            |
| held on 11.11.2025 in virtual mode. The list of participants in enclosed in Annexure-I.                                                                                  |
| In the meeting, it was informed that in view of the effectiveness of 3rd Amendment of Connectivity & GNA Regulations 2022, the transition process is to                  |
| be carried out for all applicable entities. Accordingly, the fresh connectivity applications shall be processed only after the completion of transition process.         |
| Further,  it  was  also  informed  that  for  the  applications  under  5.2  of  GNA  Regulations,  where  intimations  were  issued  before the  effectiveness  of  3rd |
| Amendment (on or before 09.09.2025), letter for submission of requisite Conn BGs was already issued on 06.11.2025.                                                       |
| In this meeting applications under 5.2 which were received before effectiveness of 3rd Amendment and not yet taken up in previous CMETS-NR meeting                       |
| were discussed. Further, one application for grant of GNARE by HZL which was deferred in 41st CMETS NR meeting was also taken up for discussion &                        |
| grant.                                                                                                                                                                   |

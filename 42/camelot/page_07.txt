| Minutes of 42nd Consultation Meeting for Evolving Transmission Schemes in Northern Region held on 11.11.2025                                                          |
| A5. Permitting maximum drawl for BESS projects with applications under 5.2:                                                                                           |
| Regarding permitting of drawl for BESS projects under 5.2, it was informed that detailed comprehensive studies shall be carried out by CTUIL depending                |
| on the drawl requirement received by all RE developers in Rajasthan at each RE pooling station and requirement of any system augmentation for drawl                   |
| are to be identified based on studies results. Therefore, maximum drawl requirement provided by the applicants shall be considered for studies & based                |
| on the outcome of studies, maximum drawl to be permitted along with requirement of any augmentation & additional Conn BGs (if applicable) shall be                    |
| intimated at the later stage to the respective applicants by CTUIL after completion of detailed system studies and deliberations in CMETS meetings.                   |
| A6. Processing of applications under 5.2                                                                                                                              |
| It was also informed that, in Rajasthan till date, about 130 GW connectivity have been received. Transmission system for evacuation of 73 GW capacity                 |
| has already been planned & approved (67GW under implementation and 6GW is under bidding). Transmission system for evacuation of balance 57GW                          |
| capacity is still under planning. In the initial phases of transmission system planning in Rajasthan, EHVAC corridors with capacity of 61 GW and a HVDC               |
| corridor for 6 GW (Bhadla-Fatehpur) were planned to connect all the available major load centers in neighboring States like Haryana, Punjab, Delhi,                   |
| Uttar Pradesh and Madhya Pradesh. Since all the nearby load centers have already been covered in the initial planning of EHVAC corridors, there are                   |
| no nearby load centers available for evacuation of additional RE power from the State of Rajasthan necessitating planning of HVDC corridors to remote                 |
| load centers/touchpoint (>1000 km) in other regions i.e. Western and Eastern Regions.                                                                                 |
| H owever,  considering  major  RE  developments  envisaged  in  Gujarat  region  (Khavda,  Bhuj  etc.),  corridors  from  Rajasthan  towards  Gujarat  are  not       |
| envisaged, constraining the planning of Tr. outlets from South-Western Rajasthan witnessing growing challenges of ROW, availability of load points and                |
| grid integration, impacting planning of transmission schemes as well as implementation timelines.                                                                     |
| As per studies, power surplus of about 30 - 55 GW across the seasons is expected in 2030-31 timeframe. The extant of surplus shall further increase                   |
| upto 85-110 GW if Green Hydrogen projects, revision of technical minimum of thermal plants from 55% to 40%, development of 50 GW storage projects                     |
| (both PSP & BESS) do not materialize.                                                                                                                                 |
| In view of the above, it has been decided that the application for 5.2 received by CTUIL shall be processed only for those entities whose transmission                |
| system associated with the connectivity granted is already finalised and approved by NCT. For those entities whose transmission system associated                     |
| with the connectivity granted is not yet approved by NCT, such applications shall be considered for grant at later stage when the transmission system                 |
| for connectivity is finalised and approved by NCT. However, for all such cases, application priority shall be maintained.                                             |
| S ince the transmission system required for the application of Vismaya Four Renewables (S. No.4 of A4) at Bhadla-IV PS has not been finalized by NCT,                 |
| accordingly,  the  application  for  additional  capacity  of Vismaya  Four  Renewables  at  Bhadla-IV  PS  shall  not  be  considered  for  grant  in this  meeting. |
| Remaining applications shall be processed in line with the 3rd Amendment of GNA regulations.                                                                          |
